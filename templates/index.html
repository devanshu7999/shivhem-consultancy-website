About Us

Shivhem Consultancy was set up on a simple idea: that tax, accounting and financial planning work better when one team understands the whole picture — not just the return that's due this month. Based in Ahmedabad, we work with individuals, professionals, and small and mid-sized businesses across Gujarat.

Instead of routing you between separate specialists for filing, audit and planning, we keep it under one roof, with one point of contact who already knows your file. It means fewer repeated explanations, and advice that actually accounts for your last decision, not just your next deadline.

We also run practical, classroom-style courses for people who want to understand their own numbers — from GST basics to hands-on bookkeeping — because a client who understands the fundamentals makes better decisions with us.

  • Senior-led, not junior-run

    Your file is reviewed by someone who has seen it before.

  • One team, seven disciplines

    Tax, audit, GST, bookkeeping, finance, insurance and investment, coordinated together.

  • Rooted in Ahmedabad

    Familiar with local business patterns, and reachable when you need to walk in.

  • Advice, then a paper trail
